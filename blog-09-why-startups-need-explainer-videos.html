The Startup Communication Problem

Startups face a unique challenge that established companies don't: nobody knows who you are. Your product might be genuinely revolutionary, but if you can't explain it quickly, clearly, and compellingly, it doesn't matter. Potential customers, investors, and partners will move on before they understand your value.

This is the startup explainer video's core job: turn strangers into believers in under 90 seconds. It answers the three questions every visitor asks when they land on your site: What is this? How does it work? Why do I need it?

"If I had to do it all over again, the explainer video would be the first marketing dollar I spent. Nothing else moved our sign-up numbers like that 90-second animation."

— SaaS Founder, WREDD Client

Explainer Videos for Investor Pitches

Investors see hundreds of pitch decks every month. A polished animated explainer video embedded in your pitch deck or investor page creates an immediate impression that your team is execution-focused and communication-savvy.

  • 66% of early-stage investors watch video pitches before agreeing to a first meeting
  • A video demonstrates product clarity — if you can explain it in 90 seconds, you truly understand it
  • Animated product demos show how your solution works without requiring a live software walkthrough
  • Video pitch pages generate 3x more investor inquiry form completions than text-only pages

Investor Tip: Use your explainer video as the opening slide of your pitch deck. It immediately communicates the problem, solution, and market opportunity before you've said a word — eliminating the most common early-meeting question: "So wait, what exactly do you do?"

Explainer Videos for Product Launches

Product launches succeed or fail on clarity and excitement. An animated explainer video is the perfect launch asset because it creates both simultaneously.

For your product launch, your explainer video should:

  • Lead with the problem your target customer experiences daily
  • Demonstrate your solution's core value in the first 20 seconds
  • Show the transformation — what life looks like after using your product
  • End with a clear, urgent call to action (sign up, start free trial, join the waitlist)

Launched on Product Hunt, posted to LinkedIn, embedded on your homepage, and sent to your email list — a single well-produced explainer video does the work of an entire launch campaign content library.

Building Instant Credibility as a New Brand

Startups face a credibility gap — you're asking customers to trust a brand they've never heard of with their time, money, or data. A professionally produced animated video immediately closes this gap by signaling that your company is serious, professional, and worthy of trust.

Compare two hypothetical startups with identical products: one has a polished animated explainer on their homepage, the other has text and a few stock photos. Which one would you trust with your credit card details? The answer is obvious — and so is the business impact.

When Should a Startup Get Its Explainer Video?

The ideal timing for a startup's first explainer video is before your public launch — ideally 4–6 weeks before, so the video is ready to deploy the moment your website goes live.

  • Pre-launch (best timing): Have the video ready for Day 1. It becomes the foundation of all your marketing.
  • Post-launch but pre-scale: If you've launched without a video and are seeing high bounce rates, get the video before spending on paid acquisition.
  • Before fundraising: Always have your explainer video polished before starting investor outreach.
  • Before a key partnership or enterprise sales push: A video makes your pitch credible before the first meeting.

FAQs

Can a pre-revenue startup afford a quality explainer video?
Yes. WREDD offers startup-friendly packages that deliver professional studio quality at a price point accessible to seed-stage companies. The key is prioritizing: a $4,000–$6,000 explainer video that increases your conversion rate by 50% can be the highest-ROI spend in your pre-revenue phase.
Should I use the explainer video for paid ads?
Absolutely. Your explainer video works as a paid social ad (especially 30–60 second cuts for Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube pre-roll), as a retargeting ad for website visitors, and as a top-of-funnel awareness video for cold audiences. One production, multiple ad formats.
What if my product is still changing?
Focus your explainer on the core problem and the value delivered — not the specific features. This "problem-centric" approach keeps the video relevant even as your product evolves. Feature updates are shown through the product, not the explainer video. Most well-made startup explainers stay relevant for 2–3 years.
