Animation style is one of the most consequential decisions in explainer video production. It communicates your brand's personality, professionalism, and audience awareness before a single word of voiceover is heard. Here are the 7 most effective styles in use by leading brands in 2026 — with clear guidance on when to use each one.

The 7 Best Explainer Video Styles

Style 1: 2D Character Animation

The most popular explainer video style in the world — and for good reason. Custom illustrated characters move through branded environments, telling your story with personality, warmth, and visual richness.

Best For

  • SaaS products, mobile apps, and consumer tech
  • Brands targeting millennials and Gen Z audiences
  • B2C companies with a friendly, approachable personality
  • Educational content and e-learning platforms

Average Cost

$4,000 – $12,000 for a 60–90 second custom production

WREDD Specialty: Our 2D character animations are built in the Zack D. Films educational storytelling tradition — bold visual metaphors, clear narrative arcs, and characters that audiences genuinely connect with.

Style 2: Motion Graphics

Sleek, data-driven animation using shapes, icons, charts, and typography. No characters — just clean, purposeful motion that communicates professionalism and precision. The dominant choice for B2B brands, enterprise technology, and financial services.

Best For

  • B2B and enterprise technology brands
  • Finance, legal, and professional services
  • Data-heavy explanations and statistics
  • Companies with formal, corporate brand identities

Average Cost

$2,500 – $8,000 for a 60–90 second production

Style 3: Whiteboard Animation

A drawing hand illustrates concepts in real time on a white background. The visual simplicity keeps viewers focused entirely on the idea being communicated — no visual distractions. Highly effective for complex explanations that need deliberate pacing.

Best For

  • Educational content, online courses, and training videos
  • Healthcare and medical explanation
  • Complex process explanations where step-by-step clarity is paramount
  • NGOs and nonprofits communicating mission and impact

Average Cost

$1,500 – $5,000 for a 60–90 second production

Style 4: 3D Animation

Three-dimensional animation brings products, environments, and characters to life with cinematic depth and realism. The highest-cost style in explainer video production, but delivers unmatched visual impact for physical products, medical devices, and architectural visualization.

Best For

  • Physical product launches and detailed product showcases
  • Medical device and pharmaceutical demonstrations
  • Architecture, real estate, and interior design
  • Manufacturing and industrial process explanations

Average Cost

$8,000 – $30,000+ for a 60–90 second production

Style 5: Educational / Storytelling Animation

This is the Zack D. Films style — driven by narrative, visual metaphors, dynamic typography, and a storytelling structure that makes even the driest information genuinely engaging. The dominant style on YouTube's educational channels and one of the most shareable formats on social media.

Best For

  • YouTube channels and educational content creators
  • Brands teaching their audience (content marketing)
  • Complex topics that need storytelling to make them accessible
  • Viral social media content campaigns

Average Cost

$3,500 – $9,000 for a 90–120 second production

Style 6: Cut-Out / Paper Animation

Inspired by South Park's pioneering aesthetic, cut-out animation uses flat, layered graphic elements that move like physical paper cutouts. It creates a distinctive, playful visual identity that stands out from smoother 2D styles. Popular with creative brands and youth-focused audiences.

Best For

  • Creative agencies, design studios, and cultural brands
  • Youth-focused consumer products
  • Educational content for children and students
  • Social media campaigns requiring visual distinctiveness

Style 7: Hybrid Live Action + Animation

Combines real footage of people, products, or environments with animated overlays, motion graphics, and visual effects. The premium approach that delivers both human authenticity and animation's explanatory power simultaneously.

Best For

  • Enterprise and premium SaaS brands
  • Investor pitches and brand films
  • Companies where both the team and the product need to be shown
  • Broadcast and TV advertising
