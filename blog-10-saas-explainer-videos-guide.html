Why SaaS Products Uniquely Need Animation

SaaS products face a fundamental marketing challenge that physical product companies don't: your product is intangible. You can't put it on a shelf, photograph it, or let customers "try before they buy" with zero friction. The features and benefits that make your software revolutionary are invisible to someone who hasn't used it.

Animation solves this entirely. A well-crafted SaaS explainer video:

  • Makes your software's value visible before the customer signs up
  • Demonstrates the user journey without requiring a product walkthrough
  • Shows the before and after — the painful status quo vs. the effortless solution
  • Handles objections and answers questions before the customer even thinks to ask them
  • Works for both technical decision-makers (CTOs) and business buyers (CEOs)

Best Animation Styles for SaaS Explainer Videos

1. 2D Character Animation (Most Popular for SaaS)

Relatable characters experiencing the problem your software solves — then experiencing the solution. Emotional narrative drives the "I need this" realization. Best for: B2C SaaS, HR tech, productivity apps, communication tools.

2. Motion Graphics with UI Animation

Shows the actual software interface in animated form, demonstrating key features through smooth motion graphics. Best for: enterprise SaaS, technical products, analytics platforms, developer tools.

3. Hybrid: Characters + UI Animation

Combines the emotional hook of character-driven storytelling with UI screen demonstrations. The most effective approach for complex SaaS products that need both emotional engagement and feature demonstration.

Writing a High-Converting SaaS Explainer Script

The SaaS explainer video script follows a specific formula that consistently outperforms alternatives:

01

The Problem (0–15 seconds)

Open with your target customer's most painful daily frustration. Be specific. "Every morning, [ICP] wastes 2 hours on [frustrating manual process]..." Make them feel seen.

02

Agitation (15–25 seconds)

Amplify why this matters. What is this problem costing them? Missed revenue? Wasted hours? Stressed team? Make the stakes clear.

03

Introduce the Solution (25–45 seconds)

Enter your product as the hero. Name it. State what it does in one clear sentence. Show the transformation — not the features, the outcome.

04

How It Works (45–70 seconds)

Show 2–3 key features or steps in the user journey. Animate the interface. Make it look effortless and intuitive. Show the specific moments of delight.

05

Social Proof + CTA (70–90 seconds)

Drop a credibility signal (users, rating, recognizable customers), then close with a single, urgent call to action: "Start your free trial today."

Using Animation for SaaS Customer Onboarding

The highest churn period for SaaS products is the first 7 days. Users who don't experience your product's core value within the first session often never return. Animated onboarding videos bridge this gap:

  • Welcome video: 60-second animated welcome that shows the "quick win" — the fastest path to experiencing your product's core value
  • Feature spotlight videos: 30–45 second animated tips highlighting underused features that drive retention
  • Milestone videos: Short animated celebrations when users hit key milestones within your product
  • Upgrade prompt videos: Animated demonstrations of premium features when users approach their plan limits

SaaS companies that use video in their onboarding report 60% fewer support tickets and 25% higher 30-day retention rates.

FAQs

Should my SaaS explainer show the actual product interface?
Yes — but animated, not screenshotted. Animated UI demonstrations look cleaner, are more controlled, and communicate the user experience more effectively than actual screen recordings. They also don't date as quickly when your UI updates. WREDD typically animates 2–3 key UI moments within a character-driven narrative.
How technical should my SaaS explainer video be?
Lead with outcomes, not features. Your explainer should make the business buyer (CEO, CMO) feel excited and the technical buyer (CTO, developer) feel confident — simultaneously. Address what the product does, not how it's built. Save technical depth for product demo videos and documentation.
How often should a SaaS company update its explainer video?
Plan for a full refresh every 2–3 years, or sooner if: your core value proposition changes, you rebrand, or you pivot to a different ICP. Minor script or voiceover updates can often be made without full re-animation.
