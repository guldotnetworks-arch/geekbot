  <h2 class="wb-h2">What <span>Drives</span> the Price?</h2>
  <div class="wb-rule"></div>
  <p class="wb-p">Understanding the variables behind explainer video pricing helps you negotiate intelligently and make smarter decisions about where to invest versus where to save.</p>

  <div class="wb-steps">
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">01</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Video Length</h3>
        <p>The most direct cost driver. A 60-second animation requires roughly 1,200–1,500 frames of illustration and animation at 24fps. Every additional 15 seconds adds meaningful labor. Most studios quote per 30 or 60 seconds of finished video. A 90-second video is not 50% more expensive than a 60-second one — the setup costs are fixed, so it is typically 20–30% more.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">02</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Animation Style and Complexity</h3>
        <p>Simple motion graphics cost less than complex character animation. A video with 3 scenes and minimal characters is dramatically cheaper than one with 10 scenes, lip-synced characters, and detailed environments. 3D animation costs 2–4× more than comparable 2D work because of modeling, rigging, rendering, and lighting complexity.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">03</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Script and Strategy</h3>
        <p>Some studios include scripting in their package; others charge separately ($300–$1,500 for a 90-second script from a qualified copywriter). A conversion-focused script is not a commodity — it is the most important creative investment in the entire production. Never accept a studio that does not have a structured scripting process.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">04</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Voiceover and Sound</h3>
        <p>Professional voiceover talent ranges from $200–$2,000+ depending on usage rights, market, language, and talent profile. Custom original music adds $300–$1,500. Licensed royalty-free music is typically included at lower price points. Sound design (ambient audio, SFX) adds polish and depth — its absence is immediately noticeable in finished videos.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">05</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Number of Revisions</h3>
        <p>Most professional studios include 2 revision rounds per production stage (script, storyboard, animation, final). Additional revision rounds are billed separately, typically $150–$500 per round. Clients who take time to brief thoroughly and approve quickly rarely need extra revisions. Those who "figure it out as we go" consistently exceed their initial budget.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">06</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Studio Location and Overhead</h3>
        <p>A studio based in New York or London carries significantly higher overhead than one operating in Karachi, Lahore, or Eastern Europe — and this is reflected in pricing. WREDD Animation Studio offers world-class production quality at rates that reflect our Pakistan base, delivering premium output at 40–60% of Western studio pricing without compromising on craft.</p>
      </div>

  <h2 class="wb-h2">Why <span>Cheap</span> Explainer Videos Cost More in the Long Run</h2>
  <div class="wb-rule"></div>
  <p class="wb-p">The temptation to minimize video production costs is understandable — especially for early-stage companies. But there is a pattern that plays out repeatedly: brands choose the lowest-cost option, receive a video that misrepresents their product or fails to convert, and ultimately spend more money either fixing it or starting over.</p>

  <ul class="wb-list">
    <li><strong>Brand damage:</strong> A low-quality video on your homepage signals low-quality everything. Visitors form instant judgments — a cheap animation can increase bounce rate and reduce trust, the opposite of the intended effect.</li>
    <li><strong>Revision spiral:</strong> Budget studios often underprice to win the project, then charge per revision. Three rounds of changes at $300 each on a $900 video is $1,800 — more than a mid-range option would have cost.</li>
    <li><strong>No source files:</strong> Cheap videos often come without editable source files. When your product updates, you cannot amend the video — you start from scratch. Professional studios provide layered project files as standard.</li>
    <li><strong>Missed conversion opportunity:</strong> Every week your homepage runs an underperforming video is a week of lost conversions. If your product converts at 2% with a great video and 0.8% with a poor one, on 10,000 monthly visitors, that is 12 lost conversions every month — at whatever your average customer value is.</li>
    <li><strong>The real cost calculation:</strong> Ask not "how much does this video cost?" but "what is the cost of a video that does not convert?" The answer reframes the investment entirely.</li>
  </ul>

  <div class="wb-quote">"The bitterness of poor quality remains long after the sweetness of low price is forgotten." The same is profoundly true in animation. A great video pays for itself many times over. A cheap one costs you more than it saved.</div>

  <h2 class="wb-h2">How to Calculate <span>ROI</span> on an Explainer Video</h2>
  <div class="wb-rule"></div>
  <p class="wb-p">Before investing in an explainer video, run this simple ROI framework to understand whether the investment makes sense for your business.</p>

  <div class="wb-steps">
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">01</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Know Your Current Conversion Rate</h3>
        <p>What percentage of homepage or landing page visitors currently convert to a lead or sale? If you do not know this number, install conversion tracking before ordering a video — you need a baseline to measure against.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">02</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Estimate the Lift</h3>
        <p>Industry benchmarks show explainer videos increase landing page conversion rates by an average of 20–80%, depending on the quality of the video and the existing page. Use a conservative 20% lift for your calculation to avoid over-estimating.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">03</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Calculate Additional Monthly Revenue</h3>
        <p>Monthly visitors × conversion lift (as decimal) × average customer value = additional monthly revenue attributable to the video. At 10,000 visitors, a 0.4% conversion lift (from 2% to 2.4%), and $200 average customer value: 10,000 × 0.004 × $200 = $8,000/month.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">04</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Divide Video Cost by Monthly Revenue Lift</h3>
        <p>A $6,000 video generating $8,000/month in additional revenue pays for itself in under one month. Even with conservative assumptions, most professional explainer videos in the right placement achieve full ROI within 2–6 months — then continue generating returns for 2–3 years.</p>

  <h2 class="wb-h2">Questions to Ask <span>Any Studio</span> Before You Hire Them</h2>
  <div class="wb-rule"></div>
  <ul class="wb-list">
    <li><strong>Can I see 5 recent examples that are similar to my project in style and industry?</strong> Generic portfolios are a red flag. The best studios have relevant case studies with measurable outcomes.</li>
    <li><strong>Who writes the script and what is your scripting process?</strong> If the answer is "we send you a template to fill in," walk away. Strategic scripting is not a form — it is a craft.</li>
    <li><strong>What happens if I need changes after final delivery?</strong> Understanding post-delivery revision policy protects you from expensive surprises when your product updates six months later.</li>
    <li><strong>Do I own the source files and all rights to the final video?</strong> Some studios retain IP or charge separately for source file handoff. Clarify upfront. You should own everything outright upon full payment.</li>
    <li><strong>What does your revision process look like and how many rounds are included?</strong> A structured, stage-gated revision process with clear approval checkpoints is a mark of a professional studio.</li>
    <li><strong>What is the realistic timeline from brief to delivery?</strong> Anyone promising broadcast-quality video in under 2 weeks is cutting corners somewhere in the process.</li>
  </ul>
