<h2 class="wb-h2">The 5-Part Product Explainer <span>Formula That Converts</span></h2>
<div class="wb-rule"></div>
<p class="wb-p">Every high-converting product video follows this structure. Each stage has a specific job. Skip or reorder any stage and the video loses conversion effectiveness.</p>

<div class="wb-steps">
  <div class="wb-step">
    <div class="wb-step-num">01</div>
    <div class="wb-step-content">
      <h3>Hook With the Problem — First 5 Seconds</h3>
      <p>You have five seconds to earn the next 60. Open with the problem your product solves — stated as a scene, a situation, or a pain the viewer immediately recognizes. Do not open with your logo, company name, or product name. The viewer's first question is "is this relevant to me?" Answer it immediately.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-step">
    <div class="wb-step-num">02</div>
    <div class="wb-step-content">
      <h3>Make the Pain Real — Seconds 6–15</h3>
      <p>Amplify the problem. Show the consequences: the wasted time, the cost, the frustration, the missed opportunity. The more vividly you can depict the world without your product, the more relief the solution will provide when you introduce it. This is where empathy creates urgency.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-step">
    <div class="wb-step-num">03</div>
    <div class="wb-step-content">
      <h3>Introduce the Product as the Solution — Seconds 16–25</h3>
      <p>Bring in your product as the answer to the pain you have just established. Name it clearly, state what it does in one sentence, and let the emotional shift from problem to solution happen visually. This is the pivot point — everything before was setup, everything after is evidence.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-step">
    <div class="wb-step-num">04</div>
    <div class="wb-step-content">
      <h3>Demo Key Benefits — Outcomes, Not Features — Seconds 26–75</h3>
      <p>This is where most product videos fail. Features describe what the product does. Benefits describe what the viewer gains. "Automated reporting" is a feature. "Get your Monday morning back" is a benefit. Show two to three key benefits with visual demonstrations. Prioritize outcomes over specifications — buyers buy results, not functions.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-step">
    <div class="wb-step-num">05</div>
    <div class="wb-step-content">
      <h3>Single, Clear Call to Action — Final 10 Seconds</h3>
      <p>End with one CTA. Not two. One. "Start your free trial," "Get a demo," "Shop now." Multiple CTAs create decision paralysis and reduce conversion. The CTA should repeat once on screen and once in the voiceover for maximum retention. Make the next step feel easy and low-risk.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<h2 class="wb-h2">Animation vs. Live Action — <span>Which Is Right for Your Product?</span></h2>
<div class="wb-rule"></div>

<div class="wb-split">
  <div class="wb-split-visual">
    <div class="wb-split-grid"></div>
    <div class="wb-split-icon">
      <div class="wb-split-icon-box">🎨</div>
      <div class="wb-split-label">Animation</div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-split-content">
    <h3>When Animation Wins</h3>
    <ul class="wb-list">
      <li><strong>Digital products and SaaS:</strong> Animation can show inside any interface, workflow, or system — live action cannot compete</li>
      <li><strong>Technical or industrial products:</strong> 3D animation shows mechanisms, internal components, and invisible processes with complete clarity</li>
      <li><strong>Abstract services:</strong> If the product is intangible, animation gives it a visual form that live action cannot</li>
      <li><strong>Global deployment:</strong> Animation is easier to localize — swap voiceover and on-screen text without reshooting</li>
      <li><strong>Evergreen content:</strong> No actors aging, no sets to rebuild — animation stays fresh for years</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="wb-split">
  <div class="wb-split-visual">
    <div class="wb-split-grid"></div>
    <div class="wb-split-icon">
      <div class="wb-split-icon-box">📷</div>
      <div class="wb-split-label">Live Action / Hybrid</div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-split-content">
    <h3>When Live Action or Hybrid Wins</h3>
    <ul class="wb-list">
      <li><strong>Physical consumer products:</strong> Tangible objects benefit from being shown in real use contexts — food, fashion, beauty, lifestyle goods</li>
      <li><strong>Trust-building content:</strong> Human faces build emotional connection that pure animation cannot replicate</li>
      <li><strong>Testimonial integration:</strong> Real customers speaking on camera carry persuasive weight that animation cannot match</li>
      <li><strong>Hybrid approach:</strong> Combine live-action product shots with animated diagrams and callouts for maximum impact</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

    <div class="wb-faq-q">How long should a product explainer video be? <span class="wb-faq-arrow">+</span></div>
    <div class="wb-faq-a">For website product pages: 60–90 seconds is the established sweet spot. Long enough to cover the full five-part formula, short enough to hold attention through to the CTA. For paid social ads: 15–30 seconds. For YouTube: up to 3 minutes if the product is complex or high-consideration. The guiding rule is that every second must serve the conversion goal — add no time without adding value.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-faq-item">
    <div class="wb-faq-q">Should my product video have a voiceover? <span class="wb-faq-arrow">+</span></div>
    <div class="wb-faq-a">Yes, for website deployment. Voiceover adds warmth, pacing, and information density that on-screen text alone cannot achieve. For social media distribution, design for silent viewing first — many viewers watch without audio — and add voiceover as an enhancement. Subtitles or on-screen kinetic text are essential for social deployment regardless of whether voiceover is included.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-faq-item">
    <div class="wb-faq-q">How much does a product explainer video cost? <span class="wb-faq-arrow">+</span></div>
    <div class="wb-faq-a">A professional 60–90 second animated product explainer from a specialized studio typically ranges from $3,500 to $8,000 depending on visual complexity, voiceover requirements, and style. 3D product visualization for physical or industrial products starts higher — typically $6,000–$15,000. When assessed against the conversion uplift a quality product video delivers, this investment typically pays back within 60–90 days for businesses with consistent traffic.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-faq-item">
    <div class="wb-faq-q">Do product explainer videos work for B2B? <span class="wb-faq-arrow">+</span></div>
    <div class="wb-faq-a">Absolutely. B2B buyers are still human beings responding to the same cognitive principles. However, B2B product videos need to address both emotional and rational decision-making: the personal benefit to the user and the business case for the economic buyer. B2B product videos are typically slightly longer (90–120 seconds) to accommodate the additional stakeholder considerations and often include ROI framing that pure B2C videos omit.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-faq-item">
    <div class="wb-faq-q">How quickly will a product video improve my conversion rate? <span class="wb-faq-arrow">+</span></div>
    <div class="wb-faq-a">Most businesses see measurable conversion improvement within the first 30 days of deployment — assuming there is meaningful traffic to the product page. The magnitude depends on your current baseline, industry, and product complexity. Simple consumer products with clear use cases often see conversion improvements of 40–60%. Complex SaaS or B2B products — where the video also addresses comprehension barriers — often see larger improvements of 60–100% or more.</div>
