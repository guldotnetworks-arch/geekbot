<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Simple Definition</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An explainer video is a short, focused video — typically 60 to 120 seconds — designed to communicate what a business does, how it works, and why customers need it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It answers the three questions every website visitor silently asks:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What is this?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>How does it work?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Why should I care?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It answers all three in under two minutes. That's the power of an explainer video.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Science Behind Why They Work</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Explainer videos are not just visually appealing. They are psychologically engineered to stick.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When your audience sees matching visuals and hears narration at the same time, the brain encodes information through two channels simultaneously. This is called dual-coding.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The result?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>65% of visual information is retained after 3 days</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>only 10% of text-only information survives the same period</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>viewers remember your brand, product, and call to action far longer</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That memory advantage is what drives conversions days and weeks after the first view.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How Long Should an Explainer Video Be?</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Length is one of the most common questions. And the answer is simpler than most people expect.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The ideal explainer video length is <strong>60 to 90 seconds</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Short enough to hold full attention. Long enough to deliver the complete message. For SaaS products or complex concepts, up to 2 minutes is acceptable.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Every second beyond 90 costs you audience. Every second under 60 risks leaving the story incomplete.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
