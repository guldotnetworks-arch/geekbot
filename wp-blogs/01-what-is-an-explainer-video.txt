It distills complexity into clarity, turning skeptical strangers into informed, ready-to-buy prospects.</p>

  <h2 class="wb-h2">What Exactly <span>Is</span> an Explainer Video?</h2>
  <div class="wb-rule"></div>
  <p class="wb-p">An explainer video is a purpose-built piece of content designed to <strong>explain a concept, product, or service as clearly and memorably as possible</strong> in a short amount of time. The word "explainer" is the key — the entire goal is clarity. Unlike brand films that evoke emotion or testimonial videos that build trust through social proof, an explainer video's primary job is comprehension.</p>

  <p class="wb-p">Explainer videos typically share four structural elements:</p>

  <ul class="wb-list">
    <li><strong>The Problem:</strong> Acknowledging the viewer's pain point or challenge — making them feel understood in the first few seconds.</li>
    <li><strong>The Solution:</strong> Introducing the product or service as the logical, compelling answer to that problem.</li>
    <li><strong>How It Works:</strong> A brief, visual walk-through of the mechanism — features shown as benefits, complexity made simple.</li>
    <li><strong>The Call to Action:</strong> A clear, single next step — visit, sign up, call, download — delivered with confidence at the end.</li>
  </ul>

  <p class="wb-p">This structure, refined over decades of direct-response marketing and adapted for the attention economy, is why explainer videos outperform almost every other format in the awareness-to-consideration stage of the customer journey.</p>

  <div class="wb-quote">"If I had more time, I would have written a shorter letter." — Blaise Pascal. The same principle applies to video. A great explainer is not shorter because it has less to say — it is shorter because it respects the viewer's time enough to say more with less.</div>

  <h2 class="wb-h2">Why Explainer Videos <span>Work</span> — The Neuroscience</h2>
  <div class="wb-rule"></div>
  <p class="wb-p">The effectiveness of explainer videos is not marketing folklore — it is grounded in how the human brain processes information. Understanding the neuroscience helps you see why this format consistently outperforms alternatives.</p>

  <div class="wb-steps">
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">01</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Dual-Channel Processing</h3>
        <p>The brain processes visual and auditory information through separate channels simultaneously. When an explainer video combines narration with animation, both channels are engaged at once — leading to significantly deeper encoding and retention compared to reading text alone, which only engages one channel.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">02</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>The Picture Superiority Effect</h3>
        <p>Humans recall images at a dramatically higher rate than words. Research shows that 72 hours after exposure, people remember roughly 10% of text they read — but up to 65% of information paired with relevant imagery. Animation amplifies this effect because every frame is designed to reinforce the message.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">03</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Cognitive Load Reduction</h3>
        <p>Complex information presented as text forces the brain to work hard to construct mental models. Animation does that work for the viewer — externalizing the mental model visually so cognitive resources are freed up for comprehension and decision-making rather than construction.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">04</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Mirror Neurons and Character Animation</h3>
        <p>When we watch characters in animated videos navigate problems and find solutions, our mirror neuron system activates — we empathize with the character and project ourselves into the scenario. This makes the value proposition feel personal and relevant, accelerating the path to conversion.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">05</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Narrative Compression</h3>
        <p>Stories are processed more holistically than lists of facts. A 90-second explainer video can deliver the emotional and logical equivalent of a 500-word article — but in a fraction of the time and with far higher retention. Narrative structure triggers the brain's story-processing mode, which is inherently more engaging than analytical reading.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

  <h2 class="wb-h2">The 5 Main Types of <span>Explainer Videos</span></h2>
  <div class="wb-rule"></div>
  <p class="wb-p">Not all explainer videos look the same — and they should not. The best format for your brand depends on your audience, budget, message complexity, and desired tone. Here are the five most effective types:</p>

  <div class="wb-cards">
    <div class="wb-card">
      <span class="wb-card-icon">🎨</span>
      <span class="wb-card-badge">Most Popular</span>
      <h3>2D Character Animation</h3>
      <p>Flat or stylized animated characters walk through your story. Versatile, approachable, and highly customizable to brand identity. Best for B2B SaaS, healthcare, education, and consumer apps. Most widely produced style at 62% of all explainer videos.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-card">
      <span class="wb-card-icon">🧊</span>
      <span class="wb-card-badge">Premium</span>
      <h3>3D Animation</h3>
      <p>Photorealistic or stylized three-dimensional environments and characters. Commands attention instantly and conveys technical sophistication. Ideal for product visualization, architecture, engineering, medical devices, and premium brand positioning. Growing 34% year-over-year.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-card">
      <span class="wb-card-icon">⚡</span>
      <span class="wb-card-badge">Best for Social</span>
      <h3>Motion Graphics</h3>
      <p>Kinetic typography, animated data visualizations, and abstract graphic elements. No characters — pure information design in motion. Dominates social media advertising and works exceptionally well for financial services, tech platforms, and data-heavy concepts where clarity beats character.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-card">
      <span class="wb-card-icon">✏️</span>
      <span class="wb-card-badge">High Retention</span>
      <h3>Whiteboard Animation</h3>
      <p>Simulated hand-drawn illustrations build the story in real time against a white background. The progressive reveal keeps viewers watching — whiteboard style shows 28% higher completion rates for complex topics. Strong fit for training content, educational platforms, and process explanations.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-card">
      <span class="wb-card-icon">🖥️</span>
      <span class="wb-card-badge">SaaS Essential</span>
      <h3>Screencast / UI Animation</h3>
      <p>Recorded software interfaces or animated UI mockups that show exactly how a product works. Removes "show me how it works" friction in the buyer journey. Non-negotiable for SaaS demos, app onboarding sequences, and any product where the UX is the differentiator.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <h2 class="wb-h2">How Long Should an <span>Explainer Video</span> Be?</h2>
  <div class="wb-rule"></div>
  <p class="wb-p">Length is one of the most debated aspects of explainer video production, and the answer depends on where the video will live and what it needs to accomplish. Here are the research-backed benchmarks:</p>

  <ul class="wb-list">
    <li><strong>30–60 seconds:</strong> Ideal for social media ads, pre-roll, and top-of-funnel awareness. Every second counts — this format demands ruthless scripting and strong visuals that work even without sound.</li>
    <li><strong>60–90 seconds:</strong> The sweet spot for homepage hero videos, landing pages, and product launches. Long enough to cover problem-solution-CTA with nuance, short enough to retain 70%+ of viewers to the end.</li>
    <li><strong>90–120 seconds:</strong> Works for mid-funnel content where the viewer is already interested and needs more information before deciding. Email sequences, product detail pages, and retargeting campaigns.</li>
    <li><strong>2–4 minutes:</strong> Reserved for complex B2B solutions, technical products, or onboarding sequences where thoroughness outweighs brevity. Viewer retention drops sharply after 2 minutes without strong pacing.</li>
    <li><strong>The golden rule:</strong> Your video should be exactly as long as it needs to be to tell the story clearly — and not one second longer. Every unnecessary scene is a viewer lost.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 class="wb-h2">What Makes a <span>Great</span> Explainer Video?</h2>
  <div class="wb-rule"></div>
  <p class="wb-p">Thousands of explainer videos are produced every week. Most are forgettable. The ones that drive real business results share a set of defining characteristics — and it starts with the script, not the visuals.</p>

  <div class="wb-steps">
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">01</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Script First, Always</h3>
        <p>Every great explainer video is built on a great script. The script defines the message, the pacing, the emotional arc, and the call to action. No amount of beautiful animation can save a poorly structured script. Budget at least 30% of your creative energy here before a single frame is drawn.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">02</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>One Message, One Video</h3>
        <p>The most common mistake brands make is trying to explain everything in one video. Great explainers are ruthlessly focused — one product, one audience segment, one core value proposition. If you have three things to explain, make three videos.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">03</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>Professional Voiceover</h3>
        <p>The voiceover is the emotional spine of your explainer video. A mismatched, robotic, or amateurish voice-over undermines even stunning visuals. Invest in professional talent whose tone, pacing, and energy match your brand personality and target audience.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">04</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>On-Brand Visual Language</h3>
        <p>Every color, character design, typography choice, and motion style should feel unmistakably like your brand. A great explainer video extends your visual identity rather than departing from it. Consistency between your video and your website creates brand recognition and trust.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-step">
      <div class="wb-step-num">05</div>
      <div class="wb-step-content">
        <h3>A Clear, Single Call to Action</h3>
        <p>Your explainer video should end with one instruction: what should the viewer do next? Start a free trial. Book a call. Download the guide. Visit the website. One clear CTA, repeated in both the voiceover and on-screen text, outperforms vague or multiple CTAs every time.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

  <h2 class="wb-h2">Frequently Asked <span>Questions</span></h2>
  <div class="wb-rule"></div>
  <div class="wb-faq">
    <div class="wb-faq-item">
      <div class="wb-faq-q">How is an explainer video different from a promotional video? <span class="wb-faq-arrow">+</span></div>
      <div class="wb-faq-a">A promotional video focuses on generating excitement and brand awareness — it is often emotional, cinematic, and less concerned with explanation. An explainer video prioritizes clarity and comprehension above all else. Its primary job is to make sure the viewer understands what your product does and why they should care. In practice, the best explainer videos blend both — they inform and inspire simultaneously.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-faq-item">
      <div class="wb-faq-q">Do explainer videos work for B2B businesses? <span class="wb-faq-arrow">+</span></div>
      <div class="wb-faq-a">Absolutely — in fact, explainer videos may be even more valuable for B2B than B2C. B2B products are often complex, abstract, or require significant explanation to demonstrate value. An explainer video gives your sales team a powerful asset to send before calls, shortens the sales cycle, and ensures every prospect arrives with a baseline understanding of your offering. B2B companies using explainer videos report 20–40% shorter sales cycles on average.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-faq-item">
      <div class="wb-faq-q">What is the ideal length for an explainer video on a homepage? <span class="wb-faq-arrow">+</span></div>
      <div class="wb-faq-a">For a homepage hero placement, 60–90 seconds is the research-backed sweet spot. This is long enough to walk through the problem, solution, key benefits, and a call to action — but short enough that a meaningful percentage of visitors will watch to the end. Videos under 60 seconds can feel rushed. Videos over 2 minutes on a homepage lose most viewers before the CTA.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-faq-item">
      <div class="wb-faq-q">Should the video have a voiceover or just on-screen text? <span class="wb-faq-arrow">+</span></div>
      <div class="wb-faq-a">For most explainer videos, a professional voiceover dramatically outperforms text-only formats in comprehension and retention — because it engages both the auditory and visual channels simultaneously. However, for social media where 85% of videos are watched without sound, always include accurate subtitles or animated captions. The gold standard is a video that works beautifully both with and without audio.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="wb-faq-item">
      <div class="wb-faq-q">How long does it take to produce an explainer video? <span class="wb-faq-arrow">+</span></div>
      <div class="wb-faq-a">A professionally produced explainer video typically takes 3–6 weeks from brief to final delivery. The timeline breaks down as: script and concept (1 week), storyboard and style frames (1 week), animation and voiceover recording (1–2 weeks), sound design and revisions (1 week). Rush timelines are possible but generally compromise quality or require premium pricing. At WREDD, our standard turnaround is 4 weeks with two revision rounds included.</div>
