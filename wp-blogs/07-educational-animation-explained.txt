<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Science Behind Why It Works</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Educational animation is not just visually appealing. It is built on proven cognitive science.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Psychologist Allan Paivio's dual-coding theory shows that the brain processes verbal and visual information through two separate channels simultaneously. When animation combines matching visuals with narration, both channels activate at once — dramatically increasing retention.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The results are remarkable:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>65% of visual information is retained after 3 days</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>only 10% of text-only information survives the same period</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>animated learning produces 89% higher test scores than text-only instruction</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>emotional engagement from animation further amplifies memory consolidation</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
