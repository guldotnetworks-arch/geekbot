<h2 class="wb-h2">The 7 <span>Explainer Video Styles</span></h2>
<div class="wb-rule"></div>

<p class="wb-p">Each of the seven styles below occupies a distinct creative and strategic space. Understanding their individual strengths is the foundation of making the right production choice for your brand.</p>

<div class="wb-cards">
  <div class="wb-card">
    <span class="wb-card-icon">🎨</span>
    <div class="wb-card-badge">Most Popular</div>
    <h3>2D Character Animation</h3>
    <p>The industry standard. Versatile, warm, and endlessly customizable to any brand voice — from playful to professional. Ideal for storytelling-driven explainers that need a relatable human (or non-human) protagonist to guide the viewer through a narrative.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-card">
    <span class="wb-card-icon">🧊</span>
    <div class="wb-card-badge">Premium</div>
    <h3>3D Animation</h3>
    <p>The premium tier. Hyper-realistic product renders, immersive environments, and cinematic character animation. Best for brands where production quality signals brand value — tech hardware, medical devices, automotive, luxury consumer goods.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-card">
    <span class="wb-card-icon">✨</span>
    <div class="wb-card-badge">Social-First</div>
    <h3>Motion Graphics</h3>
    <p>Design elements, typography, icons, and shapes brought to life with kinetic energy. The dominant format for social advertising and brand communications. Fast, bold, data-dense, and optimized for mobile consumption without voiceover.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-card">
    <span class="wb-card-icon">📝</span>
    <div class="wb-card-badge">Education</div>
    <h3>Whiteboard Animation</h3>
    <p>Illustrations drawn in real-time (or simulated) on a white surface. Uniquely effective for complex, step-by-step explanations where the act of drawing directs attention. Delivers 28% higher completion rates on difficult topics versus other styles.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-card">
    <span class="wb-card-icon">💻</span>
    <div class="wb-card-badge">SaaS</div>
    <h3>Screencast &amp; UI Animation</h3>
    <p>Animated screen recordings, polished product walkthroughs, and UI demonstrations. The go-to format for SaaS onboarding videos, feature explainers, and technical tutorials where showing the actual interface is the entire point.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-card">
    <span class="wb-card-icon">🎬</span>
    <div class="wb-card-badge">Physical Products</div>
    <h3>Stop Motion</h3>
    <p>Frame-by-frame photography of physical objects, paper cut-outs, or clay. Distinctive, tactile, and memorable in ways digital animation rarely achieves. Premium positioning for artisan, food, retail, and lifestyle brands that want to signal craft and handmade quality.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-card">
    <span class="wb-card-icon">🔀</span>
    <div class="wb-card-badge">Hybrid</div>
    <h3>Mixed Media</h3>
    <p>Combining live action footage with animated overlays, motion graphics, and visual effects. Increasingly popular with brands that want the authenticity of real footage and the explanatory power of animation — without compromising on either.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<h2 class="wb-h2">Style Selection <span>Decision Matrix</span></h2>
<div class="wb-rule"></div>

<p class="wb-p">Use this matrix to match your goals and constraints to the right style. Answer each question and tally which style appears most often in your answers.</p>

<div class="wb-steps">
  <div class="wb-step">
    <div class="wb-step-num">01</div>
    <div class="wb-step-content">
      <h3>What is your primary communication goal?</h3>
      <p>Storytelling and emotional connection → 2D Character. Data visualization and brand awareness → Motion Graphics. Product demonstration → Screencast/UI or 3D. Step-by-step education → Whiteboard. Tactile brand differentiation → Stop Motion.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-step">
    <div class="wb-step-num">02</div>
    <div class="wb-step-content">
      <h3>What is your budget range?</h3>
      <p>Entry to mid-range ($2K–$10K): 2D Character, Whiteboard, Motion Graphics, Screencast. Mid to premium ($10K–$30K): 3D Animation, Mixed Media, premium 2D. Enterprise ($30K+): Full 3D, complex 2D world-building, live action hybrid campaigns.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-step">
    <div class="wb-step-num">03</div>
    <div class="wb-step-content">
      <h3>Where will this video primarily live?</h3>
      <p>Homepage hero: 2D Character, Mixed Media, or 3D for maximum impact. Social media ads: Motion Graphics optimized for silent viewing. YouTube explainers: Whiteboard or 2D Character for extended engagement. Email and in-app: Screencast/UI animation with clean, minimal aesthetic.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-step">
    <div class="wb-step-num">04</div>
    <div class="wb-step-content">
      <h3>How quickly do you need delivery?</h3>
      <p>Under 2 weeks: Screencast/UI or simple Motion Graphics. 3–4 weeks: 2D Character, Whiteboard, Motion Graphics. 5–8 weeks: Full 2D Character with complex scenes. 8–12 weeks: 3D Animation. 6–16 weeks: Mixed Media with live action components.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="wb-step">
    <div class="wb-step-num">05</div>
    <div class="wb-step-content">
      <h3>What does your audience expect from your brand?</h3>
      <p>Friendly and approachable: 2D Character. Innovative and cutting-edge: 3D or Motion Graphics. Educational and trustworthy: Whiteboard. Premium and artisan: Stop Motion. Modern and data-driven: Motion Graphics with kinetic typography.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
