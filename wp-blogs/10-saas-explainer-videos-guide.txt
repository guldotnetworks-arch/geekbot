<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The SaaS Script Formula That Converts</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The script structure that consistently outperforms alternatives for SaaS products follows five stages:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>The problem (0–15 seconds)</strong> — open with your ICP's most painful daily frustration. Be specific. Make them feel seen.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>The agitation (15–25 seconds)</strong> — amplify why this matters. What is it costing them? Time, revenue, stress?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Introduce the solution (25–45 seconds)</strong> — enter your product as the hero. Name it. State the core value in one clear sentence.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>How it works (45–70 seconds)</strong> — show 2–3 key features or steps. Animate the interface. Make it look effortless.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Social proof and CTA (70–90 seconds)</strong> — drop a credibility signal, then close with one clear, urgent call to action.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
