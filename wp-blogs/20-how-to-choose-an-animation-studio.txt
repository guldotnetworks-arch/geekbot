<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The market in 2026 is full of studios making the same promises. Portfolio screenshots look similar at a glance. Pricing feels arbitrary. And most businesses do not know what questions to ask before committing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here is the definitive guide to choosing an animation studio that actually delivers what your brand needs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":5} -->
<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Start With the Portfolio — But Watch the Right Things</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Every studio shows their best work in their portfolio. The question is not "does this look good?" The question is "does this match what I need?"</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When reviewing a portfolio, look for:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>work in a similar industry or product category to yours</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>consistent quality across projects — not just one standout showreel piece</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>the animation style you actually need — not just the style they prefer to make</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>evidence that their videos are built to convert, not just impress</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A studio with 50 projects in your niche at good quality is more valuable than a studio with 3 breathtaking pieces from completely unrelated industries.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":5} -->
<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Evaluate the Script and Strategy Capability</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Most businesses focus entirely on visual quality when choosing a studio. The bigger determinant of video performance is script quality.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ask any studio you are considering:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>who writes the scripts — a dedicated copywriter, the client, or a generic template?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>do they research your audience and competitors before scripting?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>can they show examples of scripts alongside the finished videos?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>how do they approach calls to action and conversion optimization?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A studio that talks about conversion strategy before you mention it is a studio that understands what the video is actually for.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1460925895917-afdab827c52f?w=1024&q=80" alt="Animation studio evaluation showing portfolio review, script assessment, and client communication quality"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":5} -->
<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Communication Test</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>How a studio communicates before you hire them tells you everything about how they will communicate during production.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Red flags to watch for:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>slow responses or vague answers to direct questions</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>quoting without asking about your audience, goals, or distribution channels</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>reluctance to discuss the production process in detail</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>no clear approval checkpoints outlined in the proposal</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Green flags to look for:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>asks smart questions about your audience before mentioning price</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>explains the production pipeline clearly and specifically</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>provides a fully itemized quote with no vague "miscellaneous" line items</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>responds quickly and directly even before money is exchanged</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":5} -->
<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Understanding What Is Included in the Quote</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Price comparison between studios is meaningless without understanding what each quote actually includes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before accepting any quote, confirm:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>how many revision rounds are included at each production stage</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>whether source files are delivered or retained by the studio</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>which export formats are included — widescreen, square, vertical</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>whether the voiceover talent is included or quoted separately</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>what the music license covers — commercial use, broadcast, perpetual</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551434678-e076c223a692?w=1024&q=80" alt="Business contract review showing animation studio deliverables, revisions, and source file ownership terms"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":5} -->
<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Check Client Testimonials and Results</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Great visuals are table stakes. What separates the best studios is results.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Look for studios that can share:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>client testimonials that mention specific outcomes — conversion improvements, view counts, ROI</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>case studies showing the brief, the approach, and the measurable result</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>repeat client relationships — clients who commission multiple projects are the strongest signal of reliable quality</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":5} -->
<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The 5 Questions to Ask Every Studio</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Use these questions in every studio evaluation conversation:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>"Can you show me a project similar to mine in terms of industry and goal?"</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>"Who writes the script and what is your strategy process before scripting?"</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>"What happens if I want changes after the animation is complete?"</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>"Do I own the source files at delivery?"</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>"Can you walk me through the complete production timeline?"</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The quality of the answers tells you more than the portfolio.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
