<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Startup Communication Problem</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Website visitors make a decision within 7 seconds of landing on your page. In that time, they ask three silent questions:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>What does this company do?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Is this relevant to me?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Can I trust them?</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Without a video, most startups fail to answer all three in time. An explainer video answers all three simultaneously — before the visitor has read a single word of copy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Investor pitch pages with video generate <strong>3 times more inquiry form completions</strong> than text-only pages.</p>

<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>For Product Launches</strong></h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Product launches succeed on two things: clarity and excitement. A well-produced animated explainer video delivers both simultaneously.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your launch video should:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>open with your target customer's most painful daily problem</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>demonstrate your solution's core value within the first 20 seconds</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>show the transformation — what life looks like after using your product</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>close with a single, urgent call to action</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That one video works across Product Hunt, LinkedIn, your homepage, email list, and every investor outreach simultaneously.</p>
